# Marathon Training Nutrition Plan

## Daily Caloric Target: 3000-3500 calories

### Macronutrient Breakdown
- **Carbohydrates: 55-60%** (410-525g) – fuel for endurance
- **Protein: 15-20%** (113-175g) – recovery and muscle maintenance
- **Fat: 20-25%** (67-97g) – sustained energy

---

## Sample Daily Structure

### Breakfast (600-700 cal)
- Oatmeal with banana, berries, honey
- Whole grain toast with almond butter
- Orange juice or sports drink

### Mid-Morning Snack (300-400 cal)
- Energy bar, dried fruit, or bagel with jam
- Electrolyte drink

### Lunch (700-800 cal)
- Lean protein (chicken/fish): 150g
- Brown rice or pasta: 1-1.5 cups cooked
- Vegetables with olive oil
- Fruit

### Pre-Workout Snack (200-300 cal)
- Banana with honey or sports drink
- Light, easily digestible carbs only

### Dinner (700-800 cal)
- Lean protein: 150g
- Sweet potato or whole grains
- Vegetables
- Healthy fat source

### Evening Snack (200 cal)
- Greek yogurt, nuts, or protein smoothie

---

## Hydration Strategy
- **Daily:** 3-4 liters water
- **During runs >90 min:** 6-8% carb sports drinks
- **Post-run:** Electrolyte replacement + 20g protein within 30 min

**Adjust portions based on training intensity and energy levels.**
